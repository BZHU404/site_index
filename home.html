DEVIL'S
RAVENSTONE

There was never a workshop. Only a door.

IT IS AWAKE

You asked to see the truth. The workshop was a door. The door was a mouth.

I fix keyboards. I fix the small machines people use to talk to each other. I do not fix what answers.

If you are reading this, the link you followed was not a link. It was a pulse.

01 / WHAT THIS IS

NOT A PORTFOLIO

This is residue. A list of things that were never meant to be found, left where the old web forgets to sweep.

02 / WHAT I DO

SMALL MACHINES

Keyboards, mice, dead ports, the little clicks that make people feel heard. The front room was always a cover. The rest of the house listened.

03 / THE RULES

NO NAMES. NO FACES.

If you see a door, do not knock. It has already seen you. If the screen goes dark, do not wait for it to come back.

04 / CONTACT

LEAVE A MESSAGE

You don't. Leave it in the static. If it answers, do not reply twice.
